Episcopal Diocese of Northern Philippines
Please review the official system terms and data protection policies below before signing in to your account.

1. Authorized Personnel Access Only 01

This portal is strictly reserved for authorized clergy members and designated Diocesan Registrar personnel of the Episcopal Diocese of Northern Philippines (EDNP). Account sharing and credential disclosure are prohibited.

2. Account Security & OTP Verification 02

Users are responsible for safeguarding their login credentials and verified mobile phone. Multi-factor verification via SMS one-time PIN (OTP) is required on new devices. Single-session concurrency is strictly enforced.

3. Data Privacy & Confidentiality (RA 10173) 03

All canonical records, Ember Letters, ordination profiles, and government IDs stored in this portal are treated as strictly confidential and processed in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

4. Document Integrity & Canonical Custody 04

All submitted documents, forms, and personal details must be accurate, truthful, and authentic. Official clergy records and canonical standing remain under the authority and custody of the Diocesan Registrar.

5. Security Auditing & Monitoring 05

For accountability and administrative protection, sign-in attempts, device registrations, document submissions, and record modifications are permanently tracked in security audit logs.
